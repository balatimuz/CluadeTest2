# Growth Research Module

Research into (1) how the "₹999 → ₹9" WhatsApp-Status ad funnels make money, and (2) what
an AI / WhatsApp builder should actually sell if they want to grow with paid ads.

Triggered by this ad: `be10x.com/ai-tools-fb4b/?utm_source=Whatsapp_Status&...` (Sept 2026).

## One-paragraph summary

The ₹9 is not the product and it isn't really a discount — it's a **paid lead qualifier**. Paying ₹9
proves the person has a working UPI/card, hands over a verified phone number for WhatsApp follow-up,
fires a *Purchase* event that lets Meta's algorithm optimise for *buyers* instead of clickers, and
creates a small psychological commitment. The money is made 3–5 days later, in the last 60–90 minutes
of a "3-hour live workshop", when ~2–5% of attendees buy a ₹35,000–₹1,00,000 program. At ₹150–₹400
per registration and a ~40–50% show-up rate, that's a ~3× ROAS before sales-team and delivery costs —
which is why every edtech in India copied it, and also why lead costs have risen 5–10× and consumers
are getting wise to it.

For someone who can actually build software, the recommendation is **not** to copy the workshop model.
Use its *mechanics* (low-ticket paid pilot instead of free trial, order bump, WhatsApp nurture,
Purchase-event optimisation) to sell a **vertical WhatsApp AI agent** to one Indian SMB niche at
₹1,499–₹4,999/month, using click-to-WhatsApp ads where the demo *is* the funnel. Details in file 02.
