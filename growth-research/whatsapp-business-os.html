Product idea · uses only WhatsApp + your bot · no Odoo, no app to install

Run the whole shop from WhatsApp

A small business owner types to one WhatsApp number the way they'd text a trusted assistant — and that assistant keeps the customer list, the sales, the udhaar (credit), the stock, and the reminders. Their customers order and pay through the same number.

That is a mini CRM (who your customers are and what they owe or bought) plus a mini ERP (what you sold, what's in stock, what money came in and went out) — with WhatsApp as the only screen.

Important · about your group bot

Be careful with “groups”

A bot that sits inside a normal WhatsApp group and replies is not officially allowed. The tools that make it possible (the unofficial libraries that pretend to be a phone) are the fastest way to get a business number banned. Fine for a hobby project; not something you can charge money for and promise it works next month.

What is official in 2026:

  • Official Groups API — a verified business number can now create groups of up to 8 people, up to 10,000 groups per number. No buttons/lists inside groups, no calls. Good for: owner + 2–4 staff + the bot as the shop's back-office group. Not for customers.
  • Broadcasts and 1-to-1 chats for customers — feels personal, no 256-member limit, fully allowed, and it's where invoices and pay links belong anyway.
  • WhatsApp Flows (forms inside the chat) and Catalogue for ordering without typing.

So: keep the group idea for the owner + staff team room (“Ramesh 5 bag cement” typed by any staff member gets recorded), and serve customers one-to-one. Build only on the official WhatsApp Business API (Meta's paid, allowed way for software to send and receive WhatsApp messages). That also means it can't break when WhatsApp updates.

Honest risks

Four things that could go wrong

Habit

The owner stops typing after a week. Biggest risk. Fix: the bot must be less effort than the paper book — one line per sale, no menus, forgives typos, and the 9 pm summary rewards them every day.

Meta

Message prices change. They just did (Oct 2026). Fix: price per message internally; keep marketing broadcasts as a paid add-on, not unlimited.

Vyapar

A big player adds “talk to your app on WhatsApp”. Fix: stay narrow (traders, then coaching), be in a language they don't bother with, and own the relationship via local CAs and market associations. Small and loved beats big and generic in a niche.

Trust

Money data on a startup's server. Fix: monthly Excel/PDF export from day one (“your data is always yours”), and a clear privacy line in the first message.
