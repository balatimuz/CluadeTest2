# What Should I Sell With Paid Ads? (AI + WhatsApp builder edition)

Context: you can build software, you already have a WhatsApp app, and you're willing to spend on
ads. The question is *what* to point the ad budget at. Below: the honest options, a scoring
matrix, a recommendation, the unit economics for the recommended path, and a 90-day plan.

---

## 1. The four realistic options

### A. Copy the ₹9 workshop model (sell AI education)
Run "Learn AI/WhatsApp automation in 3 hours" at ₹9, upsell a ₹15k–₹40k course/cohort.

- **Why it's tempting:** proven, high margins, no product to maintain, you know the material.
- **Why it's a bad fit for you:**
  - It's a *sales* business, not a software business. The moat is the sales team and creative
    factory, not the content. You'd be competing with Be10x's ad budget on their home turf.
  - CPLs are already ₹300–₹1,000 in the "learn AI" niche. As a new brand with no social proof,
    yours will be higher.
  - Regulatory/reputation risk sits on *you* personally (see file 01 §6).
  - Fundamentally it monetises anxiety; if that's not you, you'll build it badly.
- **Only worth it if:** you narrow to a niche where you have real authority (e.g. "WhatsApp
  automation for clinic owners" workshop) *and* the workshop's job is to sell your software or
  service, not another course. That's option B/C's marketing channel, not a business.

### B. Vertical WhatsApp AI agent (micro-SaaS) — **recommended**
A WhatsApp-native AI assistant for **one** type of Indian SMB, priced ₹1,499–₹4,999/month, sold via
click-to-WhatsApp ads where the prospect talks to the bot itself as the demo.

- **Why it fits:** it's what you already build. India's WhatsApp API market is ~₹500 Cr/yr growing
  ~45% YoY; 2.5 lakh SMBs are migrating from unofficial tools; Indian customers *prefer* WhatsApp
  over email/apps; SMEs in 2026 will pay ₹1,500–₹5,000/mo for a tool that saves ~2 hrs/week.
- **Why vertical, not horizontal:** "WhatsApp chatbot for any business" already has 50 well-funded
  competitors (AiSensy, Wati, Interakt, Gallabox…) with ₹999 entry plans. You cannot out-market them
  horizontally. But "the WhatsApp receptionist for dental clinics in Tier-2 cities" — pre-built
  intents, Hinglish/Tamil/Telugu, appointment slots, no-show reminders, pre-consult instructions,
  Google-review nudge — is a product a generic bot builder can't ship, and a clinic owner buys it
  in one conversation because it speaks their language.
- **Candidate niches** (pick ONE; all confirmed underserved in 2026 sources):

  | Niche | Core pain the bot removes | Willingness to pay | Ease of reaching via ads |
  |---|---|---|---|
  | Clinics / dentists / physio | 18–24% no-shows; receptionist tied up on calls; missed enquiries after hours | High (₹2–5k/mo trivially recovered by 2 saved appointments) | Good — "clinic owner"/"dentist" interest targeting works |
  | Coaching institutes / tuition centres | Admission enquiries flood during season; fee reminders; batch timing Qs | High in season (Feb–Jul) | Very good — huge, ad-literate segment |
  | Salons / spas | Booking, reminders, rebooking nudges | Medium (₹1–2k/mo) | Good — Instagram-heavy |
  | Real-estate agents / small builders | Lead qualification, site-visit scheduling, brochure delivery | High (₹3–10k/mo; one deal pays for a year) | Good but crowded |
  | Kirana / D2C micro-brands | Order-taking, catalogue, delivery status | Low–medium; price-sensitive | Cheap to reach but churny |

  **Top pick: clinics (dental first), coaching institutes as the second vertical.** Both have a
  quantifiable ROI story ("we recovered X appointments/enquiries this month") that you can put in
  the ad itself.

### C. Productized service: "WhatsApp automation setup for X" (agency wedge)
Charge ₹15,000–₹50,000 one-time setup + ₹2,000–₹5,000/month retainer to set up WhatsApp API,
flows, and an AI agent for a business. Agencies doing this report ~28% margins and a path to ₹50L+.

- **Why it fits:** immediate cash flow (day-1 revenue, no pricing-page problem), and every client
  is a paid discovery interview for option B. High-ticket enough that ₹500–₹1,500 CPL is fine.
- **Why not forever:** it's time-for-money; ceiling is your calendar. Use it for the first 90–180
  days to fund B and to find out which vertical actually pays.

### D. Low-ticket digital product (₹99–₹499 prompt packs, templates, "50 WhatsApp flows")
- **Why it's tempting:** cheapest way to learn Meta ads with real Purchase events; near-zero
  delivery cost.
- **Why it's weak alone:** ceiling is low, refunds are high, and the ₹99 buyer is not the ₹3,000/mo
  buyer. Fine as an **order bump / tripwire** in front of B or C; not a business.

## 2. Scoring matrix

Scores 1–5 (5 = best for *you*, a solo technical founder with an existing WhatsApp app).

| Criterion | A. Workshop funnel | B. Vertical WA AI SaaS | C. Productized service | D. Low-ticket digital |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Fit with your skills / asset | 2 | **5** | 4 | 3 |
| Time to first revenue | 3 | 2 | **5** | 4 |
| Ad-cost tolerance (CPL you can afford) | 2 | 3 | **5** | 1 |
| Ceiling / scalability | 4 | **5** | 2 | 2 |
| Defensibility / moat | 1 | **4** | 2 | 1 |
| Regulatory & reputation risk (5 = low) | 1 | **5** | 5 | 4 |
| Recurring revenue | 1 | **5** | 3 | 1 |
| Solo-operability | 1 | **4** | 3 | 5 |
| **Total (/40)** | **15** | **33** | **29** | **21** |

**Recommendation: B as the destination, C as the wedge, D as an order bump.** Do not do A as a
business; optionally use a *true* paid workshop later as one acquisition channel for B.

## 3. What "the funnel" looks like when you apply the ₹9 lessons to a SaaS

The mechanics from file 01 §7, translated:

```
Meta ad (Click-to-WhatsApp, WhatsApp Status + IG Reels + FB Feed)
  Creative: 15s vertical video, one vertical, one pain:
  "Dr. Priya's clinic lost 23 appointments last month to no-shows. Now her
   WhatsApp confirms, reminds and rebooks automatically. Tap to see it work."
        │  Cost per conversation started: ₹15–₹60 (India 2026 benchmark)
        ▼
Prospect lands IN WHATSAPP, talking to YOUR agent configured as a demo clinic
  The demo *is* the product. Bot books a fake appointment, sends the reminder
  live, then says: "Want this on your clinic's number? Reply YES."
        │  Conversation → qualified lead: 15–30%
        ▼
₹499 "Paid Pilot" (your ₹9)
  14-day setup on their own number, done-for-you. Refundable against month 1.
  Filters tyre-kickers; fires a Purchase event to Meta; captures the number.
        │  Qualified → pilot: 20–40%
        ▼
OTO immediately after ₹499 payment
  "Add Hindi/Tamil voice-note replies + Google-review nudge for ₹999 one-time"
        │
        ▼
14 days of WhatsApp nurture (automated, from the same agent)
  Day 1 setup call · Day 3 "your first 10 enquiries handled" report ·
  Day 7 no-show recovered count · Day 12 "here's your month-1 invoice, ₹2,499"
        │  Pilot → paying: 40–60% (they've seen it work on real patients)
        ▼
₹2,499/month (clinic) · annual ₹24,999 (2 months free — real deadline: price
  rises after 100 clinics because support cost is real)
```

### Unit economics for the recommended path

| Step | Rate (conservative) | Cost |
|---|---|---|
| Cost per WhatsApp conversation | — | ₹40 |
| Conversation → qualified (replies with clinic name) | 20% | ₹200 per qualified |
| Qualified → ₹499 pilot | 25% | ₹800 per pilot (−₹499 collected → net ₹301) |
| Pilot → paying customer | 50% | **CAC ≈ ₹1,600 gross, ≈ ₹600 net of pilot fees (≈ ₹300 if the OTO also converts)** |
| Monthly price | ₹2,499 | |
| Monthly COGS (WhatsApp API msgs + LLM + hosting) | ~₹300–₹500 | see note below |
| Gross margin | ~80% | |
| **Payback** | **< 1 month** | |
| If churn = 5%/mo → avg lifetime 20 months | LTV ≈ ₹40,000 | **LTV:CAC ≈ 25:1** |

Even if every rate above is *half* as good, CAC ≈ ₹6,400 and payback ≈ 3 months — still healthy.
Compare: the workshop funnel needs a 3% close on a ₹40k ticket just to hit ~2× ROAS.

**COGS note (WhatsApp API, India, 2026):** marketing template ₹0.8631/msg, utility ₹0.1150/msg,
authentication ₹0.1150/msg, +18% GST, + BSP markup (or ~0 if you go direct via Meta Cloud API).
Since Jul 2025 billing is per *delivered message*, not per conversation. **From 1 Oct 2026 Meta
starts charging for service/utility messages inside the 24-hr window that used to be free** — build
your pricing on per-message cost, not on "conversations are free". A clinic sending 300 reminders
(utility) + 100 promos (marketing) a month costs you ~₹35 + ~₹86 ≈ ₹120 + GST in API fees. LLM
cost for ~2,000 short Hinglish turns/month on a small model is another ₹100–₹300. Margins are fine.

## 4. Why click-to-WhatsApp + Status ads specifically

- **Status is under-priced right now.** CPC ₹5–₹25 vs ₹6–₹55 on Instagram. Sources expect Status
  CPMs to normalise by H2 2026 as advertisers pile in — so the window is *now*.
- **Zero-friction demo.** The prospect never leaves WhatsApp. No landing page, no form, no app
  install. For a WhatsApp product this is the highest-fidelity demo possible.
- **Meta optimises for "conversation started" or, better, for your ₹499 Purchase event** — same
  algorithmic advantage Be10x exploits with ₹9.
- **You own the number.** Every prospect is now a WhatsApp contact you can (with opt-in) nurture
  at ₹0.86/msg instead of re-paying Meta ₹40 to reach them again.

Recommended starting budget from Indian benchmarks: **₹1,500–₹3,000/day per ad set** for stable
learning; **₹15,000–₹30,000/month minimum** to get statistically meaningful results. Scale winners
by ≤20% every 3–5 days.

## 5. 90-day plan (₹30,000–₹50,000 total ad spend)

### Days 1–14: Pick the vertical, build the demo, get 5 free pilots
- [ ] Choose **one** vertical (default: dental/general clinics in your city + 2 Tier-2 cities).
- [ ] Repurpose your WhatsApp app into a demo clinic agent: booking, 2-step reminder (24h + 2h),
      reschedule, FAQ (fees/timings/location), Hinglish + one regional language.
- [ ] Get **5 clinics to use it free** via personal network / walking in. You need real numbers
      ("recovered 11 no-shows") for the ads. No ads yet.
- [ ] Register WhatsApp Business API directly (Meta Cloud API) or via a BSP with no per-message
      markup; verify the business; get a display name approved.
- [ ] Set up Meta Pixel / Conversions API with a `Purchase` event on the ₹499 pilot payment
      (Razorpay/Cashfree webhook → CAPI).

### Days 15–45: First paid traffic (₹15–20k)
- [ ] 3 creatives × 1 vertical: (1) pain/number-led ("lost 23 appointments"), (2) demo screen
      recording of the bot, (3) doctor testimonial from a free pilot. Vertical 9:16 video, <15s,
      captions on, Hinglish.
- [ ] Campaign objective: **Sales / Conversions → WhatsApp** (not Traffic, not Leads).
      Placements: WhatsApp Status + IG Reels + FB Feed. Audience: Advantage+ with interest seed
      "dentist / clinic owner / Practo", age 28–60, your 3 cities.
- [ ] Budget ₹1,000/day. Kill any creative with cost-per-conversation > ₹80 after ₹3,000 spent.
- [ ] Offer the ₹499 pilot + ₹999 OTO. Target: **10 paid pilots**.
- [ ] Parallel (option C): offer 3 clinics a ₹15k "done-for-you WhatsApp setup" for cash flow.

### Days 46–90: Convert, learn, decide (₹15–30k)
- [ ] Convert pilots to ₹2,499/mo. Target **≥5 paying clinics** (₹12.5k MRR) and *measured*
      conversation→pilot→paid rates.
- [ ] Ship the two features pilots asked for most. Nothing else.
- [ ] Add the second creative angle (testimonial with real recovered-appointments number).
- [ ] Decision gate at day 90:
  - CAC < ₹5,000 and ≥5 paying → **scale**: ₹3k/day, add second city cluster, then second vertical
    (coaching institutes, timed for Feb–Jul admissions).
  - CAC > ₹10,000 or pilots don't convert → the vertical's wrong or the demo isn't landing; switch
    vertical, don't switch business.

### What NOT to do
- Don't build a horizontal "WhatsApp automation platform". You will lose to AiSensy's ₹999 plan.
- Don't run a free trial. Charge ₹499. Free = no show-up, no Purchase event, no commitment.
- Don't use fake timers, fake seat counts, or "3× salary" style claims. You're selling to SMBs who
  will be your customers for years and refer you — and the CCPA is actively fining exactly this.
- Don't optimise ads for Traffic or Link Clicks. Ever.
- Don't wait for the product to be "ready" before the 5 free pilots. The pilots *are* the spec.

## 6. If you still want to run a workshop
Do it as **channel, not business**: "How clinics are cutting no-shows 60% with WhatsApp AI —
45-min live session, ₹99" → the pitch at the end is *your software's* ₹499 pilot, not a ₹40k course.
Same mechanics (paid registration, WhatsApp nurture, real teaching, real offer), none of the
reputational debt. Run this only after you have 10+ paying clinics and real numbers to teach from.
